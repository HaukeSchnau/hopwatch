Privacy policy

Hopwatch doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your time stays on your phone, so this page is short.

Last updated 16 October 2026

Your entries stay on your phone

Your jellies, time entries and settings are stored in a database inside the app on your phone. They never reach us, and we couldn't see them if we wanted to.

If your phone backs up to iCloud or Google, the backup includes Hopwatch's data. That follows your backup settings and Apple's or Google's terms. Deleting the app deletes its data from the phone.

Export and restore

Export JSON in Settings creates a file with your data and opens the share sheet. Where the file goes is up to you, and we don't get a copy. Restore from Export reads only the file you pick.

Notifications, Live Activities and widgets

If you allow notifications, Hopwatch schedules its reminders on the phone itself. Nothing goes through a push server. The Live Activity on your Lock Screen, the widgets and the controls are drawn on the phone too.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Hopwatch uses Apple's on-device model, through the Foundation Models framework, to suggest looks for jellies, write the week summary and read entries you type. The model runs on your iPhone. Hopwatch sends none of this anywhere.

App updates through Expo

Apart from Slack, if you connect it (below), this is the one time Hopwatch goes online. When you open it, it asks Expo's EAS Update service whether there's a newer version of its code, like a fix or a small improvement, and downloads it if there is. Expo is a service of 650 Industries, Inc. in the USA.

The request contains:

  • technical details: the platform (iOS or Android), the app's runtime version, the update channel and the IDs of the installed updates
  • a random installation ID that the app makes up on first launch. Expo uses it to count how many installations get an update. It isn't linked to your name, your Apple or Google account or other apps, and it's gone when you delete the app
  • if the last update failed to start, a short technical error message

Like any request on the internet, it also shows Expo your IP address. It contains none of your jellies or entries.

We do this to ship fixes quickly and keep the app working, which is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Expo handles the data for us as a service provider. According to its privacy policy, Expo complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for data that reaches the USA. Details are in Expo's privacy policy.

Slack, if you connect it

Connecting Slack is optional. Until you do, Hopwatch never talks to Slack. When you connect it in Settings, you sign in on Slack's own page and allow Hopwatch to read and set your Slack status. The sign-in keys Slack hands back stay on your phone, in the iPhone's Keychain or Android's encrypted storage, and never reach us.

From then on, while a jelly with a Slack status runs, your phone sends that status straight to Slack: its text, its emoji and when it should end. Before it changes anything, it reads your current status, so it never replaces one you set yourself. Slack learns the statuses you chose, when they change and, like with any request, your IP address. Your other jellies, entries and times never go to Slack, and nothing goes through us.

Slack handles this under your workspace's terms and Slack's privacy policy. Disconnect in Settings to clear the status Hopwatch set and delete the keys from your phone. You can also remove Hopwatch from your Slack account's connected apps.

App Store and Google Play

You get Hopwatch from Apple's App Store or from Google Play, and they process data for that under their own privacy policies. If you've allowed your phone to share analytics or crash reports with app developers, Apple or Google may show us aggregated statistics and crash reports. They don't tell us who you are.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete them once the conversation is done, unless the law makes us keep them.

This website

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers, not even fonts. Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, delivers it for us. Each request reaches Cloudflare with your IP address, the time, the page, and what your browser sends along, such as its name and language. Cloudflare uses this data to deliver the site and protect it against attacks, on our behalf and under its data processing agreement. We rely on our interest in a site that works and stays secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep no access log ourselves.

Cloudflare may process this data in the United States. For such transfers it relies on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have about you, and have it corrected, deleted or restricted, or get a copy to take elsewhere. You can object to processing that relies on our legitimate interest. Write to info@urbs.one. For the data in the app you don't need us. You can see, export and erase it yourself at any time.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

Changes

If Hopwatch ever starts handling data differently, we'll update this page before that version ships and change the date at the top.
