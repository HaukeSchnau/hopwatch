Hopwatch is a time tracker you'll keep using, because switching takes one tap.

Every activity in your life is a jelly: your job, a client, deep work, the dog. Tap one and it hops into today's dial, while the one before goes back to sleep. That's the whole habit.

WHAT YOU GET
• One tap to switch. The last activity is always one tap away with "Back to".
• Forgot to switch? Start or stop "10 min ago", and Hopwatch shows exactly what changes before it does.
• Day: your day as a dial and a timeline. Tap a gap to fill it, drag an entry's edge to fix it.
• Week: totals, bubbles and target jars for weekly goals like 40 hours of work.
• Groups: jellies can live inside other jellies, like Clients › Acme › Website.
• Job hours: days off, public holidays and an overtime balance.
• Nudges: a gentle reminder when something has been running for a long time, and an evening check about gaps you didn't track.
• Switch from anywhere: the running jelly in your notifications with Stop and "Back to", a home screen widget with your pinned jellies, and a Quick Settings tile. None of them open the app.
• Shortcuts: long-press the app icon to stop, go back, or start a pinned jelly.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your entries stay on your phone, and we never see them. Hopwatch only goes online to check for app updates. Export your data as a file whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone.

In English and German.
