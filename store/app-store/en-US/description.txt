Hopwatch is a time tracker you'll keep using, because switching takes one tap.

Every activity in your life is a jelly: your job, a client, deep work, the dog. Tap one and it hops into today's dial, while the one before goes back to sleep. That's the whole habit.

WHAT YOU GET
• One tap to switch. The last activity is always one tap away with "Back to".
• Forgot to switch? Start or stop "10 min ago", and Hopwatch shows exactly what changes before it does.
• Day: your day as a dial and a timeline. Tap a gap to fill it, drag an entry's edge to fix it.
• Week: totals, bubbles and target jars for weekly goals like 40 hours of work.
• Groups: jellies can live inside other jellies, like Clients › Acme › Website.
• Nudges: a gentle reminder when something has been running for a long time.
• Live Activity: the running jelly and its timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
• Shortcuts: links to start, stop and resume for the Action Button and automations.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE, IF YOU HAVE IT
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Hopwatch suggests an emoji, a color and a look for new jellies, writes a short summary of your week, and understands entries like "2h deep work this morning". It all runs on your iPhone. Without Apple Intelligence, everything else works the same.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your entries stay on your iPhone. Export them as a file whenever you like, and restore them on a new phone.
